1. Choose a PDF

A QR code holds a small amount of data, so the file is split across a sequence of codes and reassembled when scanned. Small PDFs (roughly under 40–60 KB) work best — large files will need a lot of codes.

Lower = larger, easier-to-scan codes but more of them. If your camera struggles to read codes, try 250–350.
